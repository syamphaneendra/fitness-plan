Diet – Mon

Breakfast – Option 1

Protein Oats Bowl

40g oats cooked in water/unsweetened milk + 1 tbsp chia/flaxseed + cinnamon + handful berries

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 40g rolled oats, 200ml water or unsweetened almond/soy milk, 1 tbsp chia or flaxseed, 1/4 tsp cinnamon, handful mixed berries (blueberries/strawberries)

  1. Bring water or milk to a gentle boil.
  2. Stir in oats, reduce heat, simmer 5 min, stirring occasionally.
  3. Remove from heat, mix in chia/flaxseed and cinnamon.
  4. Top with berries and serve warm.
Breakfast – Option 2

Egg White Scramble

3 egg whites + 1 whole egg, scrambled, with berries on the side

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 3 egg whites + 1 whole egg, pinch salt & pepper, 1 tsp olive oil, handful berries

  1. Whisk eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  2. Heat oil in a non-stick pan on medium heat.
  3. Pour in eggs, scramble gently for 2–3 min until just set.
  4. Serve with berries on the side.
Lunch

Grilled Chicken, Brown Rice & Salad

150g grilled chicken breast, 1 cup brown rice, mixed salad + olive oil

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g chicken breast, 1 tsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp paprika/pepper, 1 cup cooked brown rice, salad (cucumber, tomato, lettuce), drizzle of olive oil

  1. Rub chicken with oil, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Grill or pan-sear 5–6 min per side until cooked through (no pink inside).
  3. Rest 3 min, then slice.
  4. Serve over rice with salad on the side.
Snack

Greek Yogurt & Almonds

Greek yogurt (plain, unsweetened) + 10 almonds

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g plain unsweetened Greek yogurt, 10 almonds

  1. Spoon yogurt into a bowl.
  2. Top with almonds and eat immediately.
Dinner

Baked Fish with Steamed Vegetables

150g baked fish (salmon, mackerel, rohu or pomfret), steamed broccoli + French beans (green beans)

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g salmon/mackerel/rohu/pomfret, 1 tsp olive oil, lemon juice, salt & pepper, 1 cup broccoli florets + French beans

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Rub fish with oil, lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  3. Bake 15–18 min until it flakes easily (grilling/pan-searing works just as well).
  4. Meanwhile steam broccoli and French beans 5–6 min.
  5. Serve together.

Diet – Tue

Breakfast – Option 1

Veggie Omelette with Toast

Omelette (2 whole eggs + 2 whites) with spinach/onion, 2 slices whole wheat toast

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 2 whole eggs + 2 egg whites, chopped spinach & onion, salt & pepper, 1 tsp oil, 2 slices whole wheat bread

  1. Whisk eggs with salt and pepper.
  2. Sauté spinach and onion in oil for 2 min.
  3. Pour eggs over vegetables, cook until set, fold and serve.
  4. Toast the bread and serve alongside.
Breakfast – Option 2

Protein Oats Bowl

40g oats + 1 scoop unsweetened protein powder + cinnamon + berries

Recipe (serves 1)

Same base method as Day 1 – Option 1: cook oats in water/milk, remove from heat, then stir in 1 scoop unsweetened pea/whey protein (off-heat so it doesn't clump), add cinnamon and top with berries.

Lunch

Lamb Curry with Quinoa

150g lean lamb curry (leg or loin, light oil, no cream), 1 cup quinoa, side salad

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g lean lamb (leg or loin, visible fat trimmed), 1 onion, 1 tomato, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric/coriander/cumin powder, 1 tsp oil, 1 cup cooked quinoa, side salad

  1. Heat oil, sauté onion until golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook 1 min.
  3. Add tomato and spices, cook until oil separates.
  4. Add lamb, cover and cook on low heat 25–30 min until tender, adding water as needed.
  5. Serve with quinoa and salad.
Snack

Protein Shake or Curd & Fruit

Whey/pea protein shake (unsweetened, <1g sugar) or plain curd + 1 fruit

Recipe (serves 1)

Option A: 1 scoop unsweetened protein powder + 250ml water or unsweetened milk — shake well and drink.
Option B: plain unsweetened curd topped with sliced fruit.

Dinner

Grilled Chicken with Sauteed Spinach & Carrots

150g grilled chicken, sauteed spinach + carrots

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g chicken breast, 1 cup spinach, 1/2 cup sliced carrots, 1 tsp oil, 1 clove garlic

  1. Grill or pan-sear chicken as in Day 1's lunch recipe.
  2. Sauté garlic in oil for 30 sec, add carrots, cook 4 min.
  3. Add spinach, cook until wilted.
  4. Serve alongside the chicken.

Breakfast – Option 2

Boiled Egg Whites

3 boiled egg whites + 1 whole egg + handful berries

Recipe (serves 1)
  1. Boil eggs for 9–10 min.
  2. Cool in cold water, peel, and separate extra whites if using more than one egg.
  3. Serve with a handful of berries.
Lunch

Grilled Fish, Brown Rice & Salad

150g grilled fish (tilapia or basa), 1 cup brown rice, cucumber-tomato salad

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g tilapia or basa fillet, 1 tsp oil, lemon juice, salt & pepper, 1 cup brown rice, cucumber-tomato salad

  1. Marinate fish with lemon juice, salt and pepper for 10 min.
  2. Pan-sear or grill 3–4 min per side until opaque and flaky.
  3. Serve with rice and salad.
Snack

Walnuts & Apple

Handful walnuts + 1 apple

Recipe (serves 1)

Eat as is — no cooking needed. Slice the apple if preferred.

Dinner

Chicken Stir-Fry with Peppers & Broccoli

150g chicken stir-fry with bell peppers and broccoli

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g chicken breast (sliced into strips), bell peppers, broccoli florets, 1 tsp oil, 1 clove garlic, salt & pepper

  1. Heat oil in a wok or pan on high heat.
  2. Stir-fry chicken strips 4–5 min until browned and cooked through.
  3. Add vegetables and garlic, stir-fry 4 min until crisp-tender.
  4. Season and serve.

Breakfast – Option 2

Protein Oats Bowl

40g oats + cinnamon + berries + 1 tbsp chia

Recipe (serves 1)

Same as Day 1 – Option 1.

Lunch

Lean Lamb with Mixed Vegetables & Rice

150g lean lamb (leg or loin, grilled/roasted), 1 cup mixed vegetables, small portion rice

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g lean lamb (leg or loin, trimmed of visible fat — avoid shoulder cuts/mince), mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas), small portion rice, 1 tsp oil, spices (cumin, coriander, black pepper)

  1. Marinate lamb with spices for 10 min.
  2. Grill or roast at 200°C for 20–25 min until cooked through (or pan-sear in minimal oil).
  3. Steam the mixed vegetables.
  4. Serve with a small portion of rice.
Snack

Roasted Chickpeas or Curd

Roasted chickpeas (salt & pepper) or plain curd

Recipe (serves 1)
  1. Toss boiled chickpeas with a pinch of salt, pepper and paprika.
  2. Roast in a dry pan or oven for 10 min until slightly crisp. (Or simply serve plain curd.)
Dinner

Fish with Steamed Vegetables

150g fish (tilapia or basa), steamed vegetables (light oil)

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g tilapia or basa, salt, pepper, lemon, mixed steamed vegetables

  1. Season fish with salt, pepper and lemon.
  2. Bake or pan-sear 12–15 min.
  3. Steam vegetables 6–8 min.
  4. Serve together.

150g chicken breast, 1 cup quinoa or brown rice, salad

Protein Shake with Berries

Unsweetened protein shake + a few berries

Recipe (serves 1)

Shake 1 scoop unsweetened protein powder with 250ml water or unsweetened milk. Serve with a few berries on the side.

Dinner

Fish Curry with Sauteed Greens

150g fish curry (rohu, pomfret, kingfish/surmai or salmon), sauteed spinach/kale

Recipe (serves 1)

Ingredients: 150g rohu/pomfret/kingfish (surmai)/salmon, 1 onion, 1 tomato (or puree), ginger-garlic paste, turmeric/coriander/cumin powder, 1 tsp oil, spinach or kale, garlic

  1. Sauté onion until golden, add ginger-garlic paste, cook 1 min.
  2. Add tomato and spices, cook until oil separates (keep it light — skip cream/coconut milk, or use very little).
  3. Add fish pieces, simmer gently 8–10 min until just cooked — don't overcook or it'll break apart.
  4. Separately sauté spinach/kale with a little garlic until wilted.
  5. Serve together.
